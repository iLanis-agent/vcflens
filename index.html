VcfLens

Contacts exports are opaque text files full of folding, encodings and version quirks. See every contact, phone, email and address - decoded - before you import the file anywhere.

Open VcfLens

Every version

vCard 2.1, 3.0 and 4.0 in one file? VcfLens parses each card on its own terms and tells you which versions it found.

Encodings decoded

Old 2.1 exports hide names behind quoted-printable and charsets. J=C3=BCrg becomes Jürg, automatically.

Structure, not lines

N, ORG and ADR are structured values. You get given/family names, org units and city/region/country - not raw semicolons.

Nothing uploads

The parser runs in your browser against local text. Your contacts never leave your machine.

What it reads

  • Multi-contact files, with per-contact cards and a version breakdown
  • Line unfolding (2.1/3.0 folding rules) with a folded-line count
  • Property parameters: bare types (TEL;HOME;VOICE) and TYPE= lists
  • Quoted-printable values with CHARSET=UTF-8 decoding
  • Structured N (family/given/additional/prefix/suffix), ORG units, ADR city/region/code/country
  • Escaped characters (\n, \,, \;) in FN, NOTE and ORG
  • Custom X- properties counted, photos flagged
  • Malformed input warnings: unclosed cards, content outside cards, missing VERSION

Cross-checked against vobject

The shared parser (engine.js) runs in Node against a corpus with hand-written expected facts, and every fact is independently verified by the real vobject library (python3 tests/oracle.py). If the hand-written facts and vobject disagree, the build fails. 31 checks pass (node tests/run_tests.js).
